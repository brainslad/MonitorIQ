Pushover

Pushover delivers alerts to iPhone, Android and desktop apps. It works next to e-mail: each channel has its own settings. Create an application at pushover.net (Your Applications → Create an Application) to get its token; your user key is on the pushover.net dashboard. A group key sends to every member of a delivery group.
